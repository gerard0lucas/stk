Who We Are

About Sri Takshashila Gurukul

A non-profit dedicated to empowering young minds through education, health, and holistic development—with no motive other than to serve.

Our Story

From a simple idea to a movement for change

Sri Takshashila Gurukul was born in Belagavi out of a shared belief: every child deserves access to quality education and every community deserves support that respects their dignity and potential. We started with a handful of volunteers and a clear vision—to bridge the gap between talent and opportunity, without seeking any personal gain.

Today we work across schools and villages, running free tuition, spoken English classes, robotics and STEM workshops, and life-skills programs. We also reach out to women through hoop art and livelihood initiatives, and to students and families through health awareness, cyber safety, and the Indian Space Show—because we believe development must be holistic.

We are registered as a non-profit and operate purely for the cause. Every program we run is designed to create lasting impact: building confidence, opening doors, and nurturing the next generation of thinkers and doers.

Why We Exist

We exist to close the gap between potential and opportunity

Education should be inclusive

Many talented students lack access to coaching, English, or digital skills. We offer free, structured programs so that where you come from does not decide how far you go.

No profit, only purpose

We are a non-profit in the true sense. Our only reward is seeing children excel, women gain confidence, and communities grow stronger together.

Community at the centre

We work with schools, teachers, and local leaders. Change is sustainable only when the community owns it—so we listen, partner, and grow with them.

What We Do

Our focus areas

We run programs across education, health, women empowerment, and community awareness—all free and open to those who need them most.

Education

Free tuition, spoken English, NMMS prep, STEM, robotics, and career guidance. We also run the Takshashila Talent Scholarship and support school transformation.

Health & Wellness

Growth monitoring, addiction relief support, yoga, and Satvik Dinacharya. We believe a healthy mind and body are the foundation of learning.

Women Empowerment

Hoop Art Mastery and livelihood programs that build skills and confidence. We have given exposure and training to hundreds of women.

Awareness & Outreach

Cyber security awareness, Indian Space Show for schools, and campaigns around national and global days—building informed, responsible citizens.
